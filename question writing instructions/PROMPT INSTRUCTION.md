## Prompt template

Paste this to an LLM together with the attached guide (`QUESTION_WRITING_GUIDE.md`) and the sample file (`sample_question_bank.csv`), after filling in the parts in brackets:

**Step 1: research and plan.** Follow step 1 of the guide: find the official syllabus or scope for *[Topic / Exam Name]*, use web search if you can, and check every fact against a primary source. Use the syllabus to choose the modules and knowledge points (step 2), and its weightings to decide how many questions each one gets. Tell me the plan, then stop and wait for my approval before writing any questions.

**Step 2: write the CSV (after I approve the plan).** Create a CSV file using the attached guide and `sample_question_bank.csv` as the format reference, and write a question bank that follows the approved plan, starting with the header row. In this reply, don't include your research notes, topic list or any other text.

Write at least *30* questions, at least *3* per knowledge point, with a mix of single- and multiple-answer questions and a mix of difficulty. Run through the guide's checklist.
